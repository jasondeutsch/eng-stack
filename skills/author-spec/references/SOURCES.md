# Planning skill sources

The five registry skills are scoped adaptations, not installations of either upstream methodology. Source revisions below pin the reviewed upstream material; the authoring skills' document guidance and HTML presentation are local conventions.

| Upstream | Reviewed revision | Relevant techniques |
|---|---|---|
| [Matt Pocock skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/59db609eb1e18aaf59b63284d0e52255aff0c63c) | `59db609eb1e18aaf59b63284d0e52255aff0c63c` | `to-spec`: repository-grounded synthesis and scope; `grilling`: dependent decisions and finding facts before asking; `to-tickets`: verifiable increments and dependency edges |
| [Superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/tree/2ce8aa15d05bd57213e6f746f953aafb7ded7a8b) | `2ce8aa15d05bd57213e6f746f953aafb7ded7a8b` | `writing-plans`: explicit interfaces, coverage, and proportionality; `receiving-code-review`: evaluate evidence before implementing feedback; `verification-before-completion`: evidence before completion claims; `executing-plans`: durable progress |

Deliberate deviations: no required issue tracker, Git/worktree, auto-commit, mandatory TDD, skill-triggered subagents, fixed human approval sequence, unbounded interview, or independent workflow scheduling. Findings may concern spec or plan rather than implementation. The assignment controls output paths, permissions, outcomes, and handoff. HTML replaces upstream Markdown conventions; progress is separate from design documents.

Both sources use MIT licenses. Retained notices are in [LICENSE](LICENSE). The registry does not promise compatibility with future upstream changes; review adaptations explicitly rather than updating them automatically.
