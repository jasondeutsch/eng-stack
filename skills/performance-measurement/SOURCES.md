# Source and adaptation

Adapted from pstack's [benchmark-checklist](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/b7b93c36be9bcc36a74db438e0fd385d660bc559/pstack/skills/benchmark-checklist/SKILL.md) at reviewed revision `b7b93c36be9bcc36a74db438e0fd385d660bc559`. Retained [MIT notice](LICENSE).

Preserves comparable workloads/configuration, limiter analysis, work/error counts, repeatability, correctness, and end-to-end relevance. Replaces fixed Linux tooling and unconditional conclusions with available host tools, declared uncertainty, and task-proportionate measurement. No automatic optimizer, benchmark implementation, installation, or workflow coordination is added.
